# Psoriasis multi-omics: research reconstruction and interactive R course

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

This repository brings together an independent reconstruction of the research reported in [Bioinformatics, DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/btag347](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag347) and a 13-chapter, hands-on R course.

## Choose an entry point

- **Online course:** [Open the course website](https://aka-debug-jie.github.io/psoriasis-multiomics/). Use this link after the site is published.
- **Student package:** [Release page](https://github.com/aka-debug-jie/psoriasis-multiomics/releases). The v4 packages are being prepared and are undergoing native-platform acceptance; downloads are not ready yet.
- **Research source:** Start with [research/execute.R](research/execute.R) and the analysis materials under [research/](research/). This is the independent research reconstruction, separate from the teaching examples.

## First run

When the v4 student packages are ready, choose the archive matching your operating system and processor, then extract it to a short folder path. The planned package set includes Windows, Intel Mac, and Apple silicon Mac, with Chinese and English editions. Each package will include R 4.4.2 and Jupyter, so no separate R or Jupyter installation will be needed.

Open **Run course.bat** on Windows or **Run course.command** on Mac. For a read-only preview, open **View course.html**. To run and edit lessons, open the course Notebook in Jupyter and follow Chapter 00.

The packages are still undergoing native-platform acceptance. The release page will list the downloads after that work is complete.

## Research reconstruction and scope

The research code under [research/](research/) independently reconstructs selected analyses from the cited paper. It is not an exact reproduction of the paper's full inputs, code, or every reported analysis. Some original research inputs are unavailable or replaced with public alternatives. The seven candidates were generated by the reconstruction analysis and are reported separately from the paper’s 29 candidates.

The public teaching edition makes the following data choices; the original research snapshot is preserved separately:

- The public teaching workflow uses MVP for discovery and Stuart for external validation. The preserved research workflow also includes FinnGen.
- Teaching examples use eQTL Catalogue and GTEx blood data (n=670) for ERAP2 and LIPA. These are instructional examples, not discovery candidates.
- Reactome release 92 is used in place of KEGG for the relevant analysis.
- The clinical 22-outcome MVP panel and FinnGen use different phenotype definitions.
- Individual-level UK Biobank PGS and Cox analyses were not reproduced.

The course keeps its PGS and Cox examples separate from those research analyses. The PGS lesson uses real 1000 Genomes Phase 3 genotypes to calculate a score from two loci with IL7R protein weights. The Cox lesson uses the R dataset survival::lung. These are independent teaching datasets and are not joined or presented as individual-level UK Biobank results.
